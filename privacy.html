Face-Action: Falling Bricks – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Face-Action: Falling Bricks ("the game") is a stacking game you control with facial expressions. This policy covers the web version at haitialways-pixel.github.io/face-bricks.

Camera

The game uses your device's front camera only while you play with face controls. The camera image is processed entirely on your device by an on-device face-tracking model (Google MediaPipe) to detect gestures such as blinks, head turns, mouth opening, smiles, raised eyebrows and puffed cheeks.

  • Video and images are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • No face data, face geometry, or biometric identifiers are stored or sent anywhere.
  • The camera is turned off when you pause, during rest breaks, when the game ends, or when you leave the page.

You can play with touch controls instead and never grant camera access.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

A few settings are saved locally on your device only: your best scores (face and touch, and daily challenge), unlocked brick styles, calibration values (numbers describing your neutral expression, head-turn range and gesture strength, not images), sensitivity, volume, language, preview visibility, and whether you've seen the How to Play screen. This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Network use

The game may download its files and the face-tracking model from its host or a public CDN (jsDelivr / Google). Those requests contain no personal or camera data.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: faceaction.support@gmail.com

Politique de confidentialité

Dernière mise à jour : 17 octobre 2026

La caméra frontale n'est utilisée que pendant une partie avec les commandes faciales. L'image est analysée uniquement sur votre appareil (Google MediaPipe). La vidéo n'est jamais enregistrée ni envoyée, et aucune donnée faciale ou biométrique n'est conservée. La caméra s'éteint à la pause, pendant les pauses repos, à la fin de la partie ou quand vous quittez la page. Le mode tactile fonctionne sans caméra.

Aucune donnée n'est collectée : pas de compte, d'analyse, de publicité ni de suivi. Seuls quelques réglages (records, styles débloqués, valeurs de calibrage, sensibilité, volume, langue) sont gardés sur votre appareil et ne le quittent jamais.
