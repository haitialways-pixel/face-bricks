## Full description, en-US (1974 / 4000 chars)
```
Stack bricks with your face!

Face-Action: Falling Bricks is a fast, colorful sorting game you can play with touch or hands-free with your facial expressions. A brick falls in the middle of the screen: make the gesture for its color and it flies onto its own stack. The sooner you sort it, the more points it's worth.

😀 FOUR COLORS, FOUR GESTURES
• Blue: double blink
• Pink: open your mouth
• Yellow: smile
• Green: raise your eyebrows
Your front camera reads your expressions and turns them into moves. A short calibration adapts the game to your face.

🧱 STACK, SINK, SCORE
• Every brick counts down from 20 points to 0 as it falls, so decide fast.
• Fill a stack with 10 bricks and it sinks into the ground for a bonus.
• Chain correct bricks for combos up to x5, and land 3 of the same color in a row for a burst bonus.
• Special bricks: 🌈 rainbow bricks go anywhere, ⭐ golden bricks start at 100 points, and 💣 bombs must be defused with a quick head shake (or a tap).

📅 A NEW CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
Play the daily challenge (the same bricks for everyone that day) and keep your 🔥 streak alive to unlock rewards.

🏆 CHAPTERS, BADGES & STYLES
Complete chapter missions, earn badges, and unlock brick styles: Classic, Neon, Candy and Gems. Set a new record and share your score card.

👆 TOUCH MODE
Prefer buttons? Tap the four color buttons instead. The camera is only requested if you choose face mode.

♿ COMFORT OPTIONS
Colorblind patterns (a shape on every color), English and French, sound controls, and pause at any time. Games last about 1–3 minutes, perfect for a quick break.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Face tracking runs entirely on your device. Video is never recorded or uploaded, and the app collects no data. No accounts and no ads. Everything works offline.

A fun facial workout game: get your face moving and have a blast.

Face-Action: Falling Bricks is for entertainment only and is not a medical, fitness or cosmetic product.
```

## Wording rules (health claims)
Use "a fun facial workout", "get your face moving", and "for entertainment only".
Never say or imply: "improves appearance", "anti-aging", "tones/firms your face", "reduces wrinkles", "exercise therapy", or any health or medical outcome. Play policy and FTC rules require evidence for such claims.

## Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
- **Category:** Game.
- **Violence:** No. There are no characters and nothing is harmed. A cartoon 💣 bomb brick can pop with a "BOOM" burst of particles if it isn't defused; this is abstract puzzle feedback, not violence. If the questionnaire asks about explosions or cartoon violence, answer "No violence"; at most it's mild fantasy, which still rates Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Fear/horror, sexuality/nudity, crude humor, profanity:** No.

- **Digital purchases:** No.

## Ads: none (free "Continue?")
When the last heart is lost, a "Continue?" screen offers **▶️ Continue (+1 ❤️)** (FR: ▶️ Continuer (+1 ❤️)) once per game, or **No thanks**. It is free: no ad, no countdown, and no ad SDK in the app. "Contains ads: No" is accurate.
The code keeps a `showRewardedAd()` hook (marked `TODO(AdMob)`) that currently resolves immediately. **Before adding AdMob later**, you must: switch "Contains ads" to Yes; update Data safety (AdMob collects device identifiers and diagnostics); update the privacy policy and the Continue wording; add consent (UMP) where required; and re-check target-audience answers.
